## The source: a learning session

The passage is made of the teacher's own explanations from a learning session. Each message starts with the label TEACHER and its message id in square brackets. The learner's turns, the questions and the answers are not shown.

Record the load bearing ideas of the subject that the teacher explained, and the derivations that lead from foundations to results. Prefer the idea in its most general form over a restatement of one example. Where the teacher derives a result step by step, write the derivation as chains, with the foundations as premises.

Do not record what the teacher said about the lesson itself, such as the plan, a recap, an encouragement or an instruction to the learner. Do not record anything about what the learner knows or has missed.

Every proposition needs a messageId, which is the id of the teacher message that states the idea, copied exactly from the square brackets. Use only ids that appear in this passage. A proposition without a valid id is returned to you for correction.
